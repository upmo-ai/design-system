## Upmo marks

These identify Upmo and are not covered by the AGPL:

- the name "Upmo",
- the caret mark (`content/assets/icons/caret*.svg` and the app icons in `content/assets/logos`),
- the wordmark (`content/assets/logos/upmo-wordmark-*.svg`, the Open Graph cards and the renders in `content/assets/exports`).

You may fork, change and redistribute the code under the AGPL. A fork must not use the Upmo name, caret mark or wordmark for its product, its site or its packages, and must not suggest that Upmo made, endorses or supports it. Replace the marks and the name before you publish a fork.

## What is fine without asking

Nominative use: saying what something is or works with, in plain text, without the logos. For example "a theme for Upmo", "compatible with Upmo" or "forked from the Upmo design system". Keep it factual and do not imply a partnership.

Upmo uses them only to identify the service or language they stand for (nominative use) and claims no affiliation. The rules and the source of every file are in [`content/assets/brands/README.md`](content/assets/brands/README.md) and [`content/assets/languages/README.md`](content/assets/languages/README.md).
