---
title: Principles
description: The three principles behind every token and component.
---

Upmo is a control room for an always-on AI workforce you own. The interface is a
professional operations tool: crisp, sharp-bordered, dense where data lives,
icon and text everywhere, familiar, but alive because agents work while you are
away.

## Alive, not noisy

The interface continuously and honestly shows real activity: what is working,
what is waiting on you, what it cost. Motion conveys real state, never
decoration, and degrades fully under reduced motion.

## Autonomy with a hand on the wheel

Every autonomous action is observable and interruptible. Approvals are a
first-class object. Destructive or expensive actions ask first; where it is
safe, prefer undo to confirmation.

## Yours, visibly

Your infrastructure, your providers, your keys, your data. Ownership is a felt
quality throughout the product, not fine print in settings.

## The retained floor

Accessible (4.5:1 text, never colour alone, keyboard reachable, one focus ring,
works at 200% zoom). Tokens only. Familiar words, one concept to one word.
Honest about cost and state. No emoji, sentence case, tabular numerals.
