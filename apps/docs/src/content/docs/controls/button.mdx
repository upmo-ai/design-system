The Button is a Mantine `Button` themed by `@upmo-ai/theme`: sharp 2px action
radius, emerald primary, familiar icon-and-text. This preview renders the real
component. Switch themes to confirm it holds in light, dark and high contrast,
and watch the dark primary fill stay vivid with a soft hover glow.

<ButtonSmoke client:only="react" />

## Variants

- **Filled** (default): the one primary action per region. Emerald fill.
- **Light**: a secondary action on an emerald-soft ground.
- **Default**: a bordered, neutral action.
- **Subtle**: the quietest action, no border or fill at rest.
- Destructive actions use `color="red"`, always with a word, never colour alone.
