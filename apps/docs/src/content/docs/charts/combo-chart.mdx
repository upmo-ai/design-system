title: ComboChart
description: Bars on the left axis and one line on the right axis over the same x labels.
---

`ComboChart` puts a count beside a rate: runs per day as bars and the success
rate as a line. The two series keep their own scales, so each axis has its own
ticks and a title naming what it measures. Live previews are in the
[Storybook gallery](https://design.upmo.dev/storybook/). The rules every chart
follows are on the [Charts overview](/charts/overview/).

:::note[Built on ECharts, loaded lazily]
The drawing uses Apache ECharts, fetched in its own chunk the first time a chart
mounts, so a page without a chart never loads it. Until it arrives the chart
holds its height with a quiet placeholder and nothing moves. See the
[Charts overview](/charts/overview/) for the bundle size and the hover, legend
and number rules shared by every chart.
:::

```tsx
<ComboChart
  title="Runs and success rate"
  bars={{ name: 'Runs', points: days.map((d) => ({ label: d.label, value: d.runs })) }}
  line={{ name: 'Success rate', points: days.map((d) => ({ label: d.label, value: d.rate })) }}
  formatLine={(n) => `${n}%`}
/>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `bars` | `ComboSeries` | required | `{ name, points }`. A point is `{ label, value: number or null, display? }`. Drawn against the left axis in `chart-1`. |
| `line` | `ComboSeries` | required | Same shape. Drawn against the right axis in `chart-2` as a smooth line. Matched to the bars by position. |
| `formatBar` | `(n: number) => string` | compact numbers | Left axis ticks, tooltip and bar values without a `display`. |
| `formatLine` | `(n: number) => string` | compact numbers | Right axis ticks, tooltip and line values without a `display`. |
| `title` | `string` | "Combo chart" | Names the group and captions the hidden table. |
| `height` | `number` | 220 | In px. |
| `width` | `number` | 480 | Used only while the container has no measurable width. |
| `emptyText` | `string` | "No data to show" | |
| `className` | `string` | none | |

## Axes

Both axes have 4 or 5 round ticks and include zero. The right axis is given the
same number of ticks as the left and the range is split into equal parts, so
both axes' ticks sit on the same gridlines (the right axis labels can be less
round than the left in exchange). Only one set of gridlines is drawn. The x
labels come from the longer of the two series (the bars when they are equal) and
overlapping labels are dropped. The shorter series shows gaps where it has no
point, and the hidden table lists every point of both.

## Tooltip and legend

Hovering a position shades its band and shows a tooltip with both values, each
through its own formatter, with the bars first as the axes read. The legend under
the plot is two buttons: click one, or press Enter or Space, to hide or show the
bars or the line (its axis goes with it). The last visible series cannot be
hidden, and hovering or focusing an item highlights that series.

## Gaps

A `null` bar is skipped and a `null` line value breaks the line. Neither is drawn
as zero, and the hidden table says "No data".

## Accessibility

- The legend and the axis titles name the series, so the two are never told apart
  by colour alone. In high contrast (the `hc` theme, `prefers-contrast: more` or
  forced colours) the bars also gain a fill pattern and the line shows a marker
  at every point.
- The drawing is hidden from assistive technology. A visually hidden table,
  captioned with `title`, has a row per x label and a column for the bars and one
  for the line.
- The chart is one tab stop. The arrow keys, Home and End move along the x axis;
  a readout line gives the label and both values, and the tooltip and highlight
  follow on the plot. Escape clears it.
- Animation is off for reduced motion.
