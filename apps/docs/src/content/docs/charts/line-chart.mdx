---
title: LineChart
description: One line, or several lines, areas and stacked areas on a shared x axis.
---

`LineChart` draws a trend. Pass `data` for one line, or `series` for several
lines on one x axis. Live previews are in the
[Storybook gallery](https://design.upmo.dev/storybook/). The rules every chart
follows are on the [Charts overview](/charts/overview/).

## One line

The original form is unchanged and still dependency free: `data` is a list of
points, and the chart is a sparkline-style line with a soft wash under it. Pair
it with a `StatTile` so the trend reads without the curve.

```tsx
<LineChart
  title="Spend over the last 14 days"
  data={days.map((d) => ({ label: d.label, value: d.spend }))}
/>
```

The props below that are marked "series" apply only when you pass `series`.

## Several series

:::note[Built on ECharts, loaded lazily]
The drawing uses Apache ECharts, fetched in its own chunk the first time a chart
mounts, so a page without a chart never loads it. Until it arrives the chart
holds its height with a quiet placeholder and nothing moves. See the
[Charts overview](/charts/overview/) for the bundle size and the hover, legend
and number rules shared by every chart.
:::

```tsx
<LineChart
  title="Tokens per day"
  formatValue={(n) => `${n.toLocaleString()} tok`}
  series={[
    { name: 'Input', points: days.map((d) => ({ label: d.label, value: d.input })) },
    { name: 'Output', points: days.map((d) => ({ label: d.label, value: d.output })) },
  ]}
/>
```

Every series has the same length, and the x labels come from the first series. A
point's `value` can be `null`: the line breaks there and starts again, and the
point is never drawn as zero.

Lines are smooth monotone curves by default (they never dip below or above the
data between two points), with no markers until you hover or focus a position.
Pass `curve="linear"` for straight segments.

### Tooltip and crosshair

Hovering shows a vertical pointer line and a floating tooltip that lists every
visible series with a round swatch, its name and its value, largest first, through
`formatValue`. A series with no value at that position reads "No data".

### Legend

Under the plot, a compact list of buttons. Each shows a round swatch and the
series name, and, with `legend` on, the series' **Mean** and **Max** as compact
numbers (`536k`, `2.3M`, `1.2B`) or through your `formatValue`. Means ignore `null`
points. Click an item, or press Enter or Space on it, to hide or show that
series; the axis and, for `stacked`, the totals are recomputed. A hidden item is
struck through with a hollow swatch, and the last visible series cannot be
hidden. Hovering or focusing an item highlights its series.

### High contrast

In the `hc` theme, with `prefers-contrast: more`, or with forced colours, each
line shows a marker at every point (circle, square, triangle, diamond) and the
second four series are dashed. The normal themes use neither.

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data` | `LinePoint[]` | none | One line. `{ label, value, display? }`. |
| `series` | `LineSeries[]` | none | `{ name, colorIndex?, points }`, where a point is `{ label, value: number or null, display? }`. Wins over `data`. |
| `title` | `string` | "Line chart" | Names the group and captions the hidden table. |
| `height` | `number` | 160 for `data`, 220 for `series` | In px. |
| `width` | `number` | 480 | The viewBox width for `data`. For `series` the width used only while the container has no measurable width. |
| `colorIndex` | `1` to `6` | 1 | `data` only. Each series sets its own. |
| `area` | `boolean` | true for `data` and one series, false for several | A gradient under each line, from about 25% of the series colour at the line to nothing at the baseline. |
| `stacked` | `boolean` | false | Series only. Stacks the areas. |
| `curve` | `'linear'` or `'smooth'` | `'smooth'` | Series only. Smooth never overshoots the data. In 1.5.0 the default was `'linear'`. |
| `formatValue` | `(n: number) => string` | compact numbers | Series only. Ticks, tooltip, legend and readout. Without it, ticks and legend figures read `536k`, `2.3M`, `1.2B`. |
| `legend` | `boolean` | true | Series only. Shows the legend with each series' Mean and Max. |
| `otherLabel` | `string` | "Other" | Series only. Name of the merged series. |
| `emptyText` | `string` | "No data to show" | Series only. |
| `className` | `string` | none | |

## Stacked areas

`stacked` stacks the series in order, first at the bottom, and fills each band.
It suits parts of a total, such as token consumption by input, output and tool
definitions. The axis is sized to the stack total. Values should be zero or more:
a negative value adds nothing to the stack. A missing (`null`) point in a stacked
series contributes nothing to the stack, and that band has no point at that x.

```tsx
<LineChart title="Token consumption" series={tokens} stacked />
```

There is no separate area chart: `area` on a line chart, or `stacked`, covers it.

## More than six series

The chart never needs a seventh colour. With more than six series it keeps the
five with the highest maximum value, in their original order, and sums the rest
into one "Other" series in `chart-mid`. Ties keep the earlier series, and a
series with no values ranks last. Six or fewer are drawn as given. The hidden
data table still has a column for every series you passed.

## Accessibility

- The drawing is hidden from assistive technology. A visually hidden table,
  captioned with `title`, has a row per x label and a column per series, with "No
  data" for a `null`.
- The chart is one tab stop. The arrow keys, Home and End move along the x axis;
  a readout line under the plot gives the label and every series value, and the
  tooltip and highlight follow on the plot. Escape clears it.
- The legend is a list of buttons named "(title) series", with `aria-pressed` for
  shown or hidden.
- Colour is not the only cue in high contrast (markers, dashes), and animation is
  off for reduced motion.
