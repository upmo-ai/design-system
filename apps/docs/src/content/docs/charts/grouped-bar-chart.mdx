---
title: GroupedBarChart
description: Two or three measures side by side for each group, vertical or horizontal.
---

`GroupedBarChart` compares a few measures across groups: passed, failed and
cancelled runs for each day. Live previews are in the
[Storybook gallery](https://design.upmo.dev/storybook/). The rules every chart
follows are on the [Charts overview](/charts/overview/).

:::note[Built on ECharts, loaded lazily]
The drawing uses Apache ECharts, fetched in its own chunk the first time a chart
mounts, so a page without a chart never loads it. Until it arrives the chart
holds its height with a quiet placeholder and nothing moves. See the
[Charts overview](/charts/overview/) for the bundle size and the hover, legend
and number rules shared by every chart.
:::

```tsx
<GroupedBarChart
  title="Run outcomes per day"
  groups={[
    { label: 'Mon', bars: [{ name: 'Passed', value: 42 }, { name: 'Failed', value: 3 }] },
    { label: 'Tue', bars: [{ name: 'Passed', value: 51 }, { name: 'Failed', value: null }] },
  ]}
/>
```

## Props

| Prop | Type | Default | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `groups` | `BarGroup[]` | required | `{ label, bars }`. A bar is `{ name, value: number or null, display? }`. |
| `orientation` | `'vertical'` or `'horizontal'` | `'vertical'` | Horizontal puts the group labels on the left. |
| `title` | `string` | "Grouped bar chart" | Names the group and captions the hidden table. |
| `height` | `number` | 220 vertical; grows with the groups when horizontal | In px. |
| `width` | `number` | 480 | Used only while the container has no measurable width. |
| `formatValue` | `(n: number) => string` | compact numbers | Axis ticks, the tooltip and values without a `display`. Without it, ticks read `536k`, `2.3M`. |
| `otherLabel` | `string` | "Other" | Name of the merged bar. |
| `emptyText` | `string` | "No data to show" | |
| `className` | `string` | none | |

Every group has the same bars in the same order. The bar names come from the
first group. The value axis has 4 or 5 round ticks and always includes zero. Bars
have rounded tops (rounded ends when horizontal) and are at most 28 px wide.

## Tooltip and legend

Hovering a group shades its band and shows a tooltip that lists every visible bar
with a round swatch, its name and its value, largest first, through
`formatValue`. Under the plot, a compact legend of buttons: click one, or press
Enter or Space, to hide or show that bar, and the scale follows. The last visible
bar cannot be hidden, and hovering or focusing an item highlights its bars.

## Colour and Other

Bars use `chart-1` to `chart-3` by their position in the group. If a group has
more than three bars, the first three keep their colours and the rest are summed
into one "Other" bar in `chart-mid`. The hidden data table still has a column for every bar you passed.

In high contrast (the `hc` theme, `prefers-contrast: more` or forced colours)
each bar position also gains its own fill pattern (stripes, dots and so on), so
bars are not told apart by colour alone. The normal themes are solid.

## Gaps

A `null` value skips the bar and leaves its slot empty, so the other bars keep
their place. It is never drawn as zero. The hidden table says "No data".

## Narrow containers

The chart follows its container and keeps its text at a fixed size. When group
labels would overlap they are dropped. Long labels in the horizontal layout are
shortened with an ellipsis; the tooltip, readout and hidden table keep the full
label.

## Accessibility

- The drawing is hidden from assistive technology. A visually hidden table,
  captioned with `title`, has a row per group and a column per bar.
- The chart is one tab stop. The arrow keys, Home and End move between groups; a
  readout line gives the group label and the value of every bar, and the tooltip
  and highlight follow on the plot. Escape clears it.
- Animation is off for reduced motion.
