# Hero

The landing page hero, and the reference layout for every marketing page.

It holds the site nav with the wordmark and an eyebrow in `overline` (the small line above the headline). Then comes the headline in Familjen Grotesk `hero`, with one green phrase, and a lead in `body-lg`. Below sit two `control-lg` buttons, filled first and outline second. The caret, drawn large, is the only art. A proof row of three plain facts closes it.

- The headline has 12 words at most. On phones it drops to `hero-sm` and the caret shrinks above the text.
- One layout gives two variants. upmo.ai (shown): eyebrow "Open source · managed or self-hosted", filled "Start on Upmo Cloud", outline "Install Upmo", proof row "Open source, AGPL", "Your keys stay yours", "Costs shown, never guessed". upmo.dev: eyebrow "Open source · self-hosted", filled "Install Upmo", outline "See a task run", proof row "Docker Compose, one Postgres", "Your data stays on your server", "Costs shown, never guessed".
- There is no motion beyond the landing loader. The caret draws once on load. The page scrolls down.
- See Marketing kit for the other layouts, words and assets.
